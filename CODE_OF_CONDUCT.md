# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent, and focus on what's best for the
community. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull
requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/sangaraju1988/lookml-agentops/security/advisories/new)
(mark it "code of conduct") or by contacting the maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`.
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and
may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.
